Why Generic Optimizers Fail & Silicon-Level Tuning Wins

Most generic "Windows debloaters" and "Linux speedup scripts" use blunt, destructive regex sweeps: disabling fundamental Windows services, breaking Bluetooth audio codecs, corrupting D-Bus dependencies, and leaving hardware running with uncalibrated C-states and thermal throttling micro-stutters.

Device-Base-Optimization treats every motherboard and CPU as a unique silicon architecture. By auditing PCIe ASPM interconnects, Intel HWP energy preferences, Realtek DAC power-gating timeouts, and OEM battery charge registers, the suite delivers genuine, measurable battery extension and stutter-free latency with 100% rollback safety.

🔬 Silicon-Aware Tuning
21 autonomous sectors calibrated against Intel P/E-core registers, NVMe APST microsecond latencies, and VA-API hardware decoders.
🛡️ Lithium Cell Guard
Permanent 80% charging threshold enforcement via ACPI and ThinkLMI registers, completely preventing dendrite formation.
🎵 Zero-Pop Audio
Locks snd_hda_intel power_save=0 and deploys RNNoise neural echo cancellation to eliminate DAC click/pop stutter.
🔄 100% Reversible
Every single script pairs with a clean rollback restore mechanism (restore-*.sh / Restore-*.ps1).
